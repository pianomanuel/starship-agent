Professional Summary

Organist, pianist, choir director, composer, and teacher whose primary calling is sacred music — including fifteen years as full-time Director of Music Ministries at St. Stephen's Episcopal Church, Sewickley — with a parallel career since 2011 in FDA-regulated software quality assurance as a Lead V&V and Lead SQA (Level IV) engineer on medical-device, clinical-trial, healthcare, and embedded / IoT systems. The through-line is craft under pressure: translating the end user's day-in-the-life into work that matches the field.

Core Competencies

Music, Worship & Teaching

  • Pipe organ and piano performance; choir direction for adult, men-and-boys, and university choirs; SATB rehearsal technique
  • Liturgical music direction in the Episcopal tradition (Book of Common Prayer 1979); contemporary worship-team leadership
  • Sacred choral composition and engraving (SATB and organ); arranging and program-building for touring ensembles
  • Collegiate teaching: ear training, music theory, organ studio, group keyboard; mentoring of worship leaders and young musicians

Software Quality Assurance & Regulated Systems

  • Verification & Validation (V&V) of FDA Class I and Class II medical devices and control software; full-SDLC SQA
  • Regulatory frameworks: FDA QSR 21 CFR Part 820, ISO 13485, IEC 62304, ISO 14971, IEC 60601, ISO 9001; HIPAA
  • Requirements analysis and traceability; test planning, protocol authoring and execution, dry runs, verification reports
  • Functional, negative, stress, integration, UI, performance, and embedded / IoT testing; DTR / defect capture and resolution
  • VMware ESXi virtual test labs and VM templates for unattended automated testing; back-end verification with SQL
  • QA team leadership, SQA mentoring, and developer–QA "translation" in Agile / daily-scrum environments
