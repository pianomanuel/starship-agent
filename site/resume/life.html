Broad View

I am an organist, pianist, composer, and teacher who also built a serious second career in FDA-class software quality. The through-line is craft under pressure: liturgy and rehearsal rooms, V&V labs, and now tools (Vocalis, ManuelOps) that serve musicians the way good test systems serve devices.

Falling is easy. Climbing is the work. I climb.

Music

Composition & scores

  • Gloria (SATB and organ) — my first engraved score; Book of Common Prayer (1979) Rite II text; Edition Peters–style engraving; dedicated in memory of Sir Stephen Cleobury; Newzik Pro workflow.
  • Magnificat — written and performed at the University of Miami; in active revision via Moises Pro (lyrics and chords → stems → score).
  • Aim: work worthy of an honorary doctorate, in the lane of sacred composers such as Joseph M. Martin.

Technique & repertoire

  • Organ endurance repertoire (e.g., Bach in A minor, Finlandia) with refined stop sense.
  • Pop and crossover fluency — Billy Joel, Elton John, and James Taylor energy for worship and outreach.
  • Tools: MainStage and Logic Pro (48 kHz, Spitfire Audio libraries), Newzik Pro, Moises Pro.

Tours

  • University of Miami Singers world tours to Australia and to Romania, Yugoslavia, and Austria, with a self-written half-choral / half show-choir program.
  • Outdoor tour work with John Guest — a three-city series of large parking-lot and open-air programs.

Faith & Service

  • Formed in the Anglican choral tradition; decades of worship leadership in Sewickley and Moon Township congregations — St. Stephen's, Grace Church, Christ Church at Grove Farm, and Impact Christian Church.
  • YWAM (Youth With A Mission) — Juárez, Mexico: taught myself acoustic guitar and led worship bands under pressure for crowds with lines around the corner.
  • Mentoring worship leaders today at Impact Christian Church — from the men-and-boys choir legacy to adult worship leadership.

Teaching

I am a natural-born teacher of anything to anyone — music, software quality, drawing, and flight.

  • Geneva College (adjunct, 9/2010–6/2011): ear training and music theory; year-core preparation for students who wanted to teach; organ student Daniel Favand on the pipe organ in an approximately 300-seat stage-classroom; keyboard class on nine full-size keyboards in the basement of the old Victorian music house, standing room only.
  • My method: set the textbooks aside, bring two written-out pieces you actually want to learn (any style), then co-choose one work from the approved list for a recital jury before three music department chairs and the piano chair. Every student who stayed earned an A.
  • SQA mentoring of developer and SQA teams in regulated environments.
  • Current: Rodgon "two pages a day" teach-back practice; Vocalis teacher-mode vision (student take vs. reference stem).

Art

  • Rodgon method (Rodrigo Gonzalez) — shapes → markers → overlap → eyes and expression.
  • reMarkable notebooks, Clip Studio Paint, and Procreate on iPad; building teach-from lesson packs.
  • Perspective and anatomy studies (skull, low horizon line, head studies).
