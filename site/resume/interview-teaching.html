Geneva College — Beaver Falls, PA

9/2010 – 6/2011

Adjunct Music Faculty

  • Taught freshman and sophomore core curriculum — individual (solo) ear training and music theory — the year-long core for future music teachers.
  • Taught organ, including organ student Daniel Favand, on the pipe organ in an approximately 300-seat classroom built like a performance stage.
  • Led a standing-room-only keyboard class on nine full-size keyboards in the basement of the college's old Victorian music house.
  • Student-centered method: students brought two pieces they wanted to learn, then chose one work from the approved list for a recital jury of three music department chairs and the piano chair. Every student who remained in the course earned an A.

Professional Summary

A natural-born teacher of anything to anyone. Adjunct Music Faculty at Geneva College (9/2010–6/2011), Graduate Teaching Assistant at the University of Miami, and Illinois K–12 teaching certification (Music Education, with Special Education and music therapy) through Northwestern University — plus decades of mentoring choir singers and worship leaders.

Teaching & Mentoring

  • Church music mentoring: from the men-and-boys choir legacy to the development of adult worship leaders.
